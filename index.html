Port Forwarding (WiFi Router)

You can test if your router allows automatic port forwarding via UPnP or configure it manually in the router web interface.

How to forward ports on your Router (Step by Step):

  1. Open a browser on your PC or phone and access your router (usually http://192.168.1.1 or http://192.168.0.1).
  2. Look for the section named Port Forwarding, Virtual Server or NAT.
  3. Add a rule for your Steam Deck's Local IP (192.168.x.x):
    External & Internal Port: 5520 • Protocol: UDP.
  4. (Optional): Add a rule for port 5521 with protocol UDP for server queries.
  5. Save changes on the router. Your friends can now connect using your Public IP on port 5520!
